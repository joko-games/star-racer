The game

Every race is at full speed, and it is all about keeping it: hold the line, drift through the turns, take off where the road ends.

Star Drift

Throw the car sideways into a turn. The longer you hold the slide, the bigger the boost when you straighten out.

Star Jump & Fly

Hop, then light the thruster and fly over the gaps in the road, or straight across a loop for a shortcut.

Sixteen on the grid

Quick play puts you in a race in seconds, bots filling the field. Ranked matches you against players of your level.

Star Turbo

Drifting fills the turbo meter. Three charges, each a burst past 300 km/h, to fire where it pays.

The garage

Six cars, sixteen paints, neons, trails and horns. Race to level up and win the rest.

The level editor

Lay out your own circuit in the game, try it, and publish it for every lobby to race on.

Circuits

One simple oval for now, over a city at night. Richer circuits are on their way.
